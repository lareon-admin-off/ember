Welcome to Ember

You’re all set up. Here’s a quick tour of what matters most: staying safe, getting around, and your data. It takes about two minutes.

Use the Next button or your arrow keys. You can skip at any time, and press F1 later to see this again.

Staying safe

Read the lock

The lock beside the address bar shows how safe your connection to a site is.

Green: secureThe connection to this site looks safe.
Amber: check this siteLook closely at the address before you type anything personal.
Red: unsafeDon’t enter passwords, card details or other private information here.
Staying safe

Warnings you’ll see

  • Malware and phishing: known dangerous pages are stopped before they load, and the check runs on your device.
  • Scam and adult pages: Ember warns you first. The final choice is yours.
  • Camera, microphone, location: websites have to ask before using them. Only say yes to sites you trust.
Remember: Ember’s warnings are a help, not a guarantee. If a page asks for your password or payment details and something feels off, close it.
Getting around

Your tabs live on the side

  • Tabs sit in the dock down the side. It has buttons for a new tab and a private tab.
  • Ctrl+B hides or shows the dock. Ctrl+Shift+B moves it to the other side.
  • Ctrl+L jumps to the address bar.
  • Your open tabs come back when you relaunch Ember.
  • Library: your search history and downloads in one place, with controls to turn history off or clear it.
  • Lareon Mail: open your inbox from the toolbar without leaving the page.

Know the limits

Private tabs

A private tab’s site data is erased when you close it, so cookies and logins from that tab don’t stay on your computer.

Private doesn’t mean invisible. The websites you visit, your internet provider, and your school or work network can still see your activity. Files you download in a private tab stay on your computer.
Yours to control

Your data and downloads

  • Your history, tabs and downloads list are kept on your device. You can clear them or turn history off in the Library.
  • Ember saves downloaded files to the folder you chose during setup.
  • Searches are sent to the search sources that provide the results.
  • You agreed to the Ember Terms of Service during setup.
